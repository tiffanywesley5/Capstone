Frequently asked questions

Is Phase.ai free?

The Readiness Assessment and True Cost Calculator are free. Advanced features will be available on a paid plan.

What data do I need to get started?

Just your basic product info, ingredients, packaging costs, and what you charge. We walk you through everything.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is yours. We never share or sell your information.

How is the readiness score calculated?

It is based on five pillars: pricing health, COGS clarity, SKU profitability, co-manufacturer readiness, and compliance status.
